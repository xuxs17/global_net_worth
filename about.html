About & contact

GlobalSalary is a free, playful way to compare one monthly salary with average salaries in ten countries. You can explore a few what-if scenarios and save or share a salary passport.

How it works

We convert your monthly salary to US dollars, then divide it by each country’s average monthly salary. 1× means the same as that average.

A salary multiple is not a population percentile, wealth ranking, after-tax income or cost-of-living measure. The historical salary estimates remain unverified; please treat the comparison as a rough reference.

Salary estimates: a legacy, hand-transcribed CEOWORLD 2025 reference. Original figures have not been independently verified; gross/net tax basis is unspecified.

Exchange rates older than 7 calendar days are marked as out of date.

Exchange-rate sources: Frankfurter / ECB · ExchangeRate-API

Privacy policy

Calculations stay on your device. We save language and analytics consent, never your salary. Optional analytics excludes salary. A link you choose to copy contains the salary and currency; see our privacy policy for hosting and service details.
