Privacy policy

Updated 21 October 2026

GlobalSalary is a free salary comparison tool. This page explains what stays on your device and what our service providers receive.

Your salary and shared links

Calculations happen in your browser. We do not send your salary in analytics events or save it in local storage. Copying a link deliberately includes the salary and currency. Anyone with that link can read them; the hosting service receives those query parameters when the link is opened. The page then removes them from the address before loading other scripts.

Optional Google Analytics

Only after you allow it, GA4 measures visits, calculation use, scenarios and successful sharing. Google receives browser and device information, a cookie identifier and network information used to estimate location. We send the page path, referring site, language and named actions, excluding salary, ratios and full shared URLs. Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled.

Cookies and local preferences

We save your language and analytics choice in local storage. If you allow analytics, Google may set _ga cookies to distinguish visits. Refusing analytics does not restrict the calculator or sharing. Your salary is never saved as a preference.

Hosting and fonts

Netlify hosts this site and may log requested addresses and network information such as IP addresses. Google Fonts receives network information to deliver fonts. These requests are separate from the optional analytics service.

Advertising

Advertising is not currently enabled. We plan to use Google AdSense after approval. Google and its partners may then use cookies to serve ads based on earlier visits. Before enabling ads we will update these disclosures and provide the required consent choices, including a certified consent platform where required.

Change your choice or contact us

Privacy settings lets you withdraw analytics consent. We stop subsequent tracking and remove this site’s _ga cookies; this does not erase data already received by Google. You can also clear site storage in your browser. Google controls its own retention and processing; use the provider links below for details. For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the public project issue page without posting your salary or other private information.
